# Data

The files `uniro_posts_filtered.csv` and `uniro_comments_filtered.csv` contain the 758 posts and the 9,759 comments of the filtered extraction. The files `uniro_posts_unfiltered.csv` and `uniro_comments_unfiltered.csv` contain the 1,050 posts and the 14,283 comments of the unfiltered extraction. Both extractions cover the posts published on reddit.com/r/uniRO between January 2024 and January 2026. The names of the authors are replaced with codes of the form `user_NNNN`, and the mentions of users in the texts are replaced with `u/anon`.

The file `survey_responses.csv` contains the answers of the 75 respondents to the closed-ended questions of the survey, in Romanian. Each column name gives the type of question and the criterion, for example `likert_courses` or `rank_professors`.
